About DailyBite

DailyBite is a free, daily-updated guide to the best healthy food deals in the United States. Every morning, we re-check current offers from an approved roster of quality and healthier chains (Chipotle, Chick-fil-A, Sweetgreen, CAVA, Panera, Starbucks, sushi and poke chains, grocery prepared-food counters, and 25+ more) against official brand sources and reputable deal trackers, then publish only the deals we can verify. Chains that don't meet the healthy bar (McDonald's, Taco Bell, and the like) are deliberately excluded, no matter how good their promos look: this site reflects how its owner actually eats.

Who runs this

DailyBite is an independent, one-person site built and run by Jacob Elsayed. There's no content farm behind it: the deal pipeline is automated, but every guide, editorial rule, and deal-inclusion standard on this site is his. It is not affiliated with any restaurant chain.

How deals are verified

Our automated research pipeline runs every day at roughly 7:00 AM Eastern. It searches official brand deal pages and established deal-tracking publications, discards anything it cannot confirm as currently active, and rebuilds every page on this site. If a deal can't be verified, it doesn't get published: simple as that. Every check is logged publicly in our verification log. Offers can still vary by location, so always confirm in the restaurant's own app before ordering.

Why we built this

Food prices keep climbing, and the best discounts now hide inside a dozen different restaurant apps. DailyBite pulls them into one page so you can check once each morning and eat cheaper. A companion mobile app is in the works.

How the site makes money

DailyBite is free to use and runs no display ads. We may earn a commission when you sign up for a delivery or cash-back app through a clearly marked partner link, at no extra cost to you. Deal listings themselves are never sponsored: every Get deal link goes straight to the restaurant's own site or app, and commissions never influence which deals are listed or featured. Details live on the Privacy & Disclosures page.

What makes DailyBite different

Three editorial rules set this site apart from coupon aggregators. First, every deal is re-verified every single morning: nothing runs on autopilot from last month. Second, we exclude entire categories other sites happily list: deals locked behind paid subscriptions (DashPass, Uber One), rewards-member-exclusive offers, and anything requiring a membership to claim: if it's on DailyBite, anyone can walk in and get it. Third, we publish original research guides: birthday freebies across 47 chains, app sign-up freebies ranked, the honest fast-food-vs-groceries math: written and fact-checked for this site, not scraped.
