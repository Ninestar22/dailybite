Every deal is checked before it reaches your phone

1Checked every morningAn automated run at about 7 AM Eastern re-checks every deal against the chain's own app, website, newsroom or weekly ad.
2Unverifiable deals are droppedIf a price or end date can't be confirmed from an official source that day, the deal doesn't publish. The list shrinks rather than pads.
3Tested in personThe owner checks grocery sushi counters and chain offers in store and corrects the record when the sign disagrees with the website.
4Logged in publicEvery morning's count is in the public verification log, dated, since August 2026.

Questions, answered

Is the DailyBite app free?

Yes. The app is free on the App Store, has no ads and no paid tier, and never charges for a deal. DailyBite earns a commission only from clearly marked partner links on the website, and those never affect which deals are listed.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and today's deals are there. There is no sign-up, no email address and no password. Deals that require a paid membership, a first-order promo or a targeted offer are excluded, so everything in the list is claimable by anyone.

Is it the same list as the website?

Yes, exactly. The app reads the feed that builds dailybitedeals.com each morning, so the deals, prices and expiry dates match. The app adds distance, open-or-closed status and one-tap ordering.

Why does it ask for my location?

To sort deals by how far away they are and to tell you whether the nearest location is open right now. Location is only used for that sorting.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The website shows the same verified list in any browser, and you can filter it by city or ZIP. If an Android app ships, it will be announced on the homepage.

Which chains are in the app?

37 healthier chains and grocery counters: fast-casual bowls and salads such as Chipotle, Sweetgreen, CAVA and Panera, smoothie and juice chains, sushi and poke, Mediterranean and grilled chicken, and grocery prepared-food counters such as Publix, Kroger, Safeway and Harris Teeter. Burger, wing, pizza and dessert chains are excluded by name, and that list is public on the About page.
