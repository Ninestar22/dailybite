Grocery Store Sushi Days: $5 Sushi, by Day of the Week

Most big grocery chains run one day a week when fresh-made sushi from the in-store counter drops to about $5: usually a third to half off the everyday price. We verify these weekly and list each one in the daily deal feed on its day. Prices and participation vary by store and division, and rolls sell out: go early, and check your store's weekly ad.

Good to know: most grocery sushi counters are run by dedicated sushi companies (AFC, Snowfox, Zenshi, Oumi, Hissho) and rolls are made fresh that day, not factory-packed.
