Verdicts

Most food quality per dollar: grocery sushi days

A fresh-made roll at $5 is a third to half off its everyday price, made that morning in-store. The catch is the calendar: it's Wednesdays at most chains, Fridays at Safeway and Harris Teeter. The full weekly map lives on our sushi days page.

Most reliable any day, anywhere: Panera Mix & Match

No calendar, no code, no app: a real half sandwich or soup with a free side at $4.99, every day, nationally. Pair any two items for a full meal under $10. This is the standing deal our tracker verifies most consistently.

Cheapest real meal, full stop: Trader Joe's

The $3.49 Chicken Burrito Bowl undercuts every drive-thru box while being an actual meal. TJ's never discounts, so this is the everyday price: the whole under-$6 lineup is in our Trader Joe's guide.

Best hot meal out West: El Pollo Loco

Fire-grilled chicken combos at roughly $5 beat every fried-chicken box on both price and protein. West & Southwest only, which is exactly why our deal list labels regions honestly.

The math nobody does

A typical fast-casual lunch runs $10–$14. Swapping in any option above four times a week keeps $100+ a month in your pocket without eating out of a cardboard box. That's the entire thesis of this site.

And the $5 burger boxes? They exist, they're heavily advertised, and we deliberately don't cover them: DailyBite lists deals only from healthier, quality chains. If you searched for them, the options above are what we'd spend the same five dollars on.

Prices move. Sushi-day pricing varies by store division, Sub of the Day pricing varies by region, and combos rotate: today's verified versions are always on the homepage.
