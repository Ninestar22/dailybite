# DailyBite 30-day social calendar (Oct 19 to Nov 17, 2026)

Prepared 2026-10-19 for @dailybitedeals (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest) and dailybitedeals.com. Free iPhone app: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dailybite-healthy-food-deals/id6802622518

**Ground rules used throughout**

- Every price, store, region and rule below is taken from a live page on the site (`/sushi-deals`, `/food-deals-by-day`, `/cheap-healthy-meals`, `/free-food-today`, `/birthday-freebies`, `/publix-5-sushi-wednesday`, `/safeway-5-friday-sushi`, `/verification-log`, `/about`, `llms.txt`) or from `deals.json` as of its 2026-10-18 refresh. Nothing is invented. Where a post needs the day's deal, the slot reads **[INSERT: today's Top Pick from dailybitedeals.com: brand, deal, "save ~$X", region]**. Copy it from the homepage card that morning; never from memory.
- Deals in `deals.json` are verified as of the `updated` date and can end afterwards. Before posting any specific deal, confirm it is still on the homepage that morning.
- Pinterest is automatic (one pin set per day: up to four one-deal pins plus one evergreen explainer pin chosen by weekday). The calendar lists Pinterest only where a manual action adds something; otherwise "auto".
- Instagram and TikTok are manual and capped at **10 minutes per post by one person**. Section 0 has the production recipes that make that cap real.
- Facts the site is built on and that every post can lean on: deals are re-verified each morning at about 7 AM Eastern against the chain's own app, site, newsroom or weekly ad; the owner tests deals in person; the site never lists burger, wing, pizza or dessert chains, paid-membership perks (DashPass, Uber One), first-order-only promos, or targeted app offers not everyone can claim; regional deals are labeled with their real footprint.

| Format | Minutes | Steps |
|---|---|---|

| **Reel / TikTok (talking or walking)** | 8 to 10 | 1 min: read script. 3 min: film 3 clips of 5 to 10 s each on the phone (no retakes past two). 3 min: in the IG or TikTok editor, add the on-screen text per beat and auto-captions. 1 min: paste caption and hashtags from the calendar. 1 min: cross-post natively to the other platform (re-upload the clean export, not the watermarked one). |

| **Carousel (IG)** | 10 | One reusable 4:5 template (white background, brand accent, the existing pin typography). Slide 1 = hook, slides 2 to 5 = one fact each, last slide = CTA + URL. Type the slide text from the calendar, export, post. |
