# Draft explainers (batch 3): Chipotle Rewards, Subway Sub Club (formerly MVP Rewards), Potbelly Perks

Drafted 2026-10-20 for `const EXPLAINERS = [` in `scripts/build.mjs` (or `EXTRA_EXPLAINERS` in `scripts/explainers-extra.mjs`).
Each object matches the existing shape (slug, brand, chainSlug, day, title, h1, desc, answer, facts, faq, more) and
ends with the "Official sources checked" paragraph. All three are `day: null` pages. A paste-ready copy of the three
objects is in `marketing/drafts/explainers-rewards-3.mjs` (`export const MORE = [...]`, passes `node --check`); the
code blocks below are sliced from that file, so the two are identical.

**Verification note.** The sandbox's outbound proxy refused CONNECT to chipotle.com, subway.com and potbelly.com
(HTTP 403; WebFetch also failed DNS), so none of these pages could be fetched directly. Every fact below was instead
checked against the official page's indexed text via domain-restricted search (official domains only: chipotle.com,
newsroom.chipotle.com, ir.chipotle.com, subway.com, newsroom.subway.com, restaurants.subway.com, potbelly.com). No blogs
or third-party sources were used, and Subway's test/staging subdomains (swuat.test, swdev, swqe, order-preview) were
treated as non-official and ignored. Before publishing, a human should open each URL in the Sources tables once and
eyeball the numbers; anything that could not be confirmed on an official page is listed under "Could not verify" and
was left out of the copy.

**Two things the brief assumed that the official pages contradict:**

1. **Subway.** subway.com's U.S. rewards page is now titled "Join Sub Club & start earning!" and the U.S. terms are
   "Subway Sub Club Terms of Use"; Subway's January 7, 2026 newsroom release says Sub Club launched in December 2025.
   The Pro/Captain/All-Star tiers (MVP Rewards, September 2023) no longer appear on Subway's live U.S. page or terms;
   they survive only on Canadian pages, the 2023 launch release and test domains. The page keeps the requested slug
   `subway-mvp-rewards` (good for search intent) but is written about Sub Club, with MVP tiers covered as the former
   scheme. If you would rather have a `subway-sub-club` slug, change only the slug.
2. **Potbelly.** The tiers are **Rookie, Pro and Boss Level**, not Bronze/Silver/Gold. Nothing on potbelly.com uses
   Bronze/Silver/Gold. The copy uses the official names.

Site rules applied: no paid memberships are pitched (none of the three has one); first-order and welcome offers
(Chipotle's chips & guac, Subway's 250 sign-up points, Potbelly's free Original after first order) are described in
one neutral sentence each.
