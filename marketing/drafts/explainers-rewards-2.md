# Draft explainers: Starbucks Rewards, Chick-fil-A One, MyPanera

Drafted 2026-10-20 for `const EXPLAINERS = [` in `scripts/build.mjs` (or `EXTRA_EXPLAINERS` in
`scripts/explainers-extra.mjs`). Each object matches the existing shape (slug, brand, chainSlug, day, title, h1, desc,
answer, facts, faq, more) and ends with an "Official sources checked" paragraph. All three are `day: null` pages. The
same three objects are in `marketing/drafts/explainers-rewards-2.mjs` as `export const MORE = [...]`, which passes
`node --check`.

**Verification note.** The sandbox's outbound proxy refused direct connections to www.starbucks.com, www.chick-fil-a.com
and www.panerabread.com (DNS resolution failed: `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`), so none of these pages could be fetched
directly. Every fact below was instead checked against the official page's indexed text via domain-restricted search
(official domains only: starbucks.com, about.starbucks.com, investor.starbucks.com, chick-fil-a.com, panerabread.com,
www-beta.panerabread.com). No blogs or third-party sources were used. Before publishing, a human should open each URL in
the Sources tables once and eyeball the numbers; anything that could not be confirmed on an official page is listed
under "Could not verify" and was left out of the copy.

**The brief vs. the official pages.** Three of the brief's assumptions are out of date and the copy follows the pages:

- Starbucks no longer pays Stars by payment method. Since March 10, 2026, the rate is set by status (Green 1, Gold 1.2,
  Reserve 1.7 Stars per $1). The 25/100/200/300/400 tiers still exist; a 60-Star tier was added and the old 50 and 150
  tiers were dropped. The page explains the change rather than the old 2-vs-1 split.
- Chick-fil-A One's Red tier starts at 4,000 points, not 5,000 (Silver 1,000, Signature 10,000, all per calendar year).
- MyPanera is no longer visit-based. Panera's August 2026 release and current terms describe a points program (10 points
  per $1, 250 to 2,000 points per item). The page explains the switch and describes the old visit-based system only as
  history.

Site rules applied: no paid memberships are pitched (Unlimited Sip Club is described in neutral terms, with price, and
the page says it is not needed for points); first-purchase and welcome offers are described in one neutral sentence or
omitted (Panera's 500-point welcome offer gets one sentence; Starbucks and Chick-fil-A have none on their pages).

### Could not verify (left out of copy)
- Whether a registered physical Starbucks Card scanned at the register (without the app) still earns Stars at the status rate. The current Terms describe in-store earning "by scanning the App"; the copy says scan the app.
- Whether Free Mod Mondays is available at Green. The FAQ says all members, a press release describes it as a new Green benefit, but the rewards page lists it under Gold and Reserve. The copy says "members" without a tier; a human should check the live rewards page.
- When status resets. The FAQ says status "will reset ... when the year comes to an end"; the Terms describe a rolling 12-month window from the date the level was reached. The copy follows the Terms.
- The dollar cap on the 25-Star customization (one search summary said "$1"); not confirmed in the Terms text that surfaced, so the copy gives no dollar figure.
- Whether the 60-Star $2-off excludes anything beyond gift cards (FAQ says any item excluding gift cards; Terms say "qualifying item"). Copy uses "qualifying item".
- Any receipt-based or after-the-fact way to claim missed Stars. No official page describes one; the copy says so.
- Whether licensed stores (grocery, airports) earn at the same rate. Official pages say "participating stores"; not addressed in copy.
- The "Last updated" / effective date printed on the Terms of Use page. Not retrieved; the copy cites the March 10, 2026 launch date from the newsroom instead.
- An "upcoming terms" page (starbucks.com/rewards/upcomingterms/) is indexed. Whether it differs from the current Terms could not be checked; a human should compare before publishing.
