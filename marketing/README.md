# DailyBite marketing kits (2026-10-19)

Produced by a team of specialist agents in one session; every kit is ready to use and every
number in them was computed from this repo's data files. Nothing here posts or spends by
itself. Start with the first three.

| File | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `app-store-listing.md` | ASO kit: title and subtitle options, 100-char keyword field, full description, promo text, What's New template, screenshot captions, preview shot list, review-prompt rules | Paste into App Store Connect (fill the "current" column first) |
| `paid-ads-kit.md` | Channel ranking for $300 to $500 a month, Apple Ads (3 campaigns, 30 keywords, bids), Pinterest ads, Reddit, Instagram, one Google guard campaign, UTM scheme, GoatCounter events, weekly budget rules, kill criteria | The first paid test; the site now fires `deal/<brand>` and `app/install` click events |
| `social-calendar-30-days.md` | 33-row calendar (Oct 19 to Nov 17), 10 Reel/TikTok scripts, 20 evergreen Pinterest pins with URLs, bios, hashtags, Sunday measurement routine | Daily posting in under 10 minutes |
| `pr-outreach-kit.md` | AP-style press release, six personalised pitch emails, journalist-request templates, Product Hunt launch kit, App Store featuring nomination, DC press angles, contact directory | Outreach to the GROWTH.md targets |
| `site-pages-copy.md` | Source copy for `/app` and `/press` (both now live in the build), with the claims that still need confirming marked `[CONFIRM]` | Reference; edit the pages in `scripts/build.mjs` |
| `drafts/explainers-rewards.md` | CAVA, Sweetgreen, Tropic Rewards pages: per-fact official sources and what was left out | Review before merge; pages live via `scripts/explainers-extra.mjs` |
| `drafts/explainers-grocery.md` | Albertsons $5 Friday, Food Lion sushi, Smoothie King (not used: main already has it) | Same |
| `drafts/seasonal-pages.md` | Holiday gift-card bonuses page, Whole Foods window renewal, Boorito 2026 wording | Same; swap in the Boorito blurb when Chipotle posts 2026 terms |

Verification caveat that applies to every draft: this session's network could not open chain
and grocer websites directly, so facts were checked against the indexed text of official pages
(official domains only, no blogs). Each page links its sources; open each once before merging.
