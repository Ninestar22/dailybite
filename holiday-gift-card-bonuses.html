Holiday Gift Card Bonuses at Healthy Chains (2026)

By Jacob Elsayed, re-verified against official sources each morning

In short: Which healthier chains add a bonus card or promo code when you buy a holiday gift card: Chipotle, Panera, Qdoba, Noodles & Company and others. What each offered last year, what is announced for 2026, and the fine print on bonus cards.

As of October 19, 2026, no healthy chain has announced its 2026 holiday gift card bonus yet. Last year the pattern was consistent: Chipotle gave a $10 code to the first 20,000 online buyers of $50 in gift cards (November 24 to 28, 2025); Panera and Qdoba gave a $10 bonus card per $50 (November through December 31, 2025); and Noodles & Company gave a $10 bonus card per $50 (November 5 to December 31). Bonus cards are claimable by anyone, no membership needed, but they expire fast, usually in January or February. This page lists only announced offers with the chain's own terms, and is updated as 2026 offers go live.

Questions people ask

Has Chipotle announced its 2026 holiday gift card bonus?

Not as of October 19, 2026. Chipotle's holiday gift card page shows a $10 first-order code for new Build Your Own customers (code TRYBYOC, through December 31, 2026), which is not a gift card bonus. In 2025 the gift card offer went live November 24 and was capped at the first 20,000 purchases, so it sold out quickly.

Do I need a rewards account or membership to get a bonus card?

No. Bonus cards and codes come with the gift card purchase itself. Some chains, including Chipotle, email the code and require an online purchase from the chain's own gift card store; in-restaurant gift card purchases did not qualify in 2025.

When can I use the bonus card?

Usually not during the holidays. Panera's bonus cards were redeemable January 1 to 31, 2026; Qdoba's expired February 28, 2026; Chipotle's $10 code expired December 15, 2025. Read the dates on the bonus card before gifting it.

Are bonus cards the same as gift cards?

No. The gift card itself does not expire at Chipotle, Panera, Qdoba, Jamba or Noodles. The bonus is a promotional card or code with its own expiration date and exclusions, commonly no third-party delivery orders and no buying more gift cards with it.

Which chains do not do bonus cards?

DailyBite found no gift card purchase bonus on the official sites of CAVA, Sweetgreen, Smoothie King or Tropical Smoothie Cafe for the 2025 or 2026 holidays. If one appears, it will be added here with the chain's terms.

Where do these deals show up on DailyBite?

Announced bonus-card offers are listed on the Black Friday & Cyber Monday page and in the daily feed on the day they go live, each with a link to the chain's own terms.
