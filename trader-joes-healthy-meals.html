Trader Joe's Healthy Meals Under $6

The no-deals store that beats most deals: real shelf prices for the healthiest grab-and-go meals, verified against summer 2026 store lineups.

Written by Jacob Elsayed · Updated August 26, 2026 · Prices vary slightly by region and rotate seasonally

Why is a deals site writing about a store with no deals? Because Trader Joe's never runs one: no sales, no coupons, no loyalty card, no app, ever. That is company policy. Instead, everyday prices are set low enough that a fresh, healthy grab-and-go lunch routinely costs less than a fast-food combo. If you have a TJ's nearby, the meals below are the standing "deal" that never expires.

The under-$6 combo plays

  • Protein + kit, $5.69 to $5.89 per person: the frozen Chicken Burgers ($3.79 for two) over any $3.79-average salad kit makes two real meals for about $11.50 total.
  • The $6.98 dinner for two: Classic Greek Salad + Chicken Burrito Bowl, split family-style.
  • Office week for $22: five different grab-and-go salads averages under $4.50 a day: cheaper than one delivery order.

Fine print worth knowing

  • Prices shown are summer 2026 shelf prices; TJ's prices are identical all week (no sale days to time) but can differ by roughly 30 to 50 cents between regions.
  • The prepared case rotates seasonally: a salad may vanish for a season and return. The burrito bowl and Caesar are year-round staples.
  • No card, app, or membership exists: the shelf price is the whole story, which is exactly the kind of honesty we like.
  • TJ's has a famously generous return policy: if a prepared item disappoints, they refund it, opened or not.
