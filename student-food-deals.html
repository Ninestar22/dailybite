Do chains have actual student discounts?

A few do (Chick-fil-A and Subway at participating locations, usually 10% with student ID): but they're franchise-by-franchise and rarely stack with app deals. The short answer: app deals beat student discounts almost every time. Ask at your local spot once, then default to the apps.

The grocery counter is the real cheat code

The best-kept student secret isn't a restaurant at all: it's the prepared-food counter at the grocery store near campus. Weekly sushi days run $5, rotisserie chickens stretch across three meals, and Trader Joe's grab-and-go case beats every drive-thru on price per real meal.
